## Why this is a separate document from the marketing plan

`marketing/plan.md`'s beachhead ICP is **consumer** — people running their own multi-system self-reflection practice. This document is about a genuinely different audience: **working professional astrologers and numerologists**, who'd use Aureon not to read their own chart but to serve *their own clients* with it. That's the original vision behind tonight's build (client roster, PDF/print reports, full house-system and chart-type parity, the Practitioner pricing tier) — and it was the one item on the original roadmap never started. This is that start.

Different audience means a different motion: not content/SEO/community (§4 of the marketing plan), but direct, personal, one-to-one outreach — closer to B2B sales than consumer marketing. Nothing here gets sent by me; every message is a draft for Gio to personalize, review, and send himself. Outreach is inherently a "real person judges whether this specific person wants to hear from us" activity — it shouldn't be automated or sent at volume without a human actually meaning it.

## Positioning: why a working astrologer would actually switch

Not "try our app" — that's the consumer pitch. The practitioner pitch has to answer one question a professional actually cares about: **does this make my client work faster, cleaner, or more credible?**

**The honest answer, as of tonight's build:**

- **Every chart type in one place.** Natal, Vedic (sidereal, nakshatras, current Mahadasha), solar return, secondary progressions, composite and Davison relationship charts, synastry — a practitioner currently juggling astro-seek for calculations, a separate Vedic tool, and a third thing for progressions can do all of it in one product.
- **A real client roster**, not a spreadsheet. Save every client's birth data once, pull up any chart type for them on demand.
- **PDF/print reports** they can actually hand to a client — not a screenshot of a browser tab.
- **Things astro-seek doesn't have at all**: Human Design (cross-validated against an independent reference during build — see engineering notes if a technically-minded prospect asks), Tarot, Chinese zodiac, an AI chat layer that can hold a running conversation about a specific client's chart rather than a static report.
- **Every house system a professional would actually ask for** (Placidus, Koch, Equal, Whole Sign, Campanus, Regiomontanus, Porphyry) — not just tropical Placidus by default.

**What we are not claiming:** more accurate calculations than astro-seek (both use the same underlying Swiss Ephemeris astronomy) — the pitch is breadth-in-one-place and a nicer working surface, not "we do the math better." Overclaiming accuracy to a professional who can trivially fact-check it against their own tools would burn credibility with the one audience least tolerant of it.

**Price honesty:** Practitioner tier is $99/mo ($990/yr). A working astrologer paying for astro-seek's various paid tiers plus a separate scheduling/client tool already spends real money — the pitch is consolidation, not "cheaper than free."

## Who to actually reach, and where

Real professional astrologers congregate in specific, findable places — this isn't a guess, it's the same kind of research the Reddit Keyword Research (marketing plan §12) idea already calls for, just aimed at a professional audience instead:

- **Professional associations**: AFA (American Federation of Astrologers), ISAR (International Society for Astrological Research), NCGR (National Council for Geocosmic Research) — all have member directories, newsletters, and often a members-only forum or Facebook group. Direct outreach to association leadership about a member-benefit mention is a real, legitimate channel, not spam.
- **r/astrology's professional-leaning threads** and any dedicated "astrology business" subreddits — different register than the consumer beachhead's threads, worth a separate pass rather than assuming the same content works for both audiences.
- **Astrology conferences** (in-person and virtual) — a genuinely strong fit once there's a live product and real screenshots to show, per the marketing plan's own Q3+ conference-speaking note (§12, item 70) — but a sponsor table or lightning demo, not a keynote, is the realistic ask at this stage.
- **Independent astrologers with their own newsletter/Substack/YouTube** — the same "small newsletter writers and podcasters" outreach already planned in the consumer plan (§4 Move 5), but pitched on the practitioner angle specifically when the writer is themselves a working astrologer, not a hobbyist.
- **Gio's own existing network**, if any — the single highest-conversion channel for any B2B-shaped outreach is someone who already knows and trusts the sender. Worth an honest personal inventory before cold outreach.

## Message drafts

All of these are **drafts for Gio to read, personalize with something specific to the actual recipient, and send himself** — a templated-feeling cold message is exactly what will read as spam to a professional community that's small enough to notice.

### 1. Cold DM/email to an individual working astrologer

> Subject: A working tool built for exactly what you do
>
> Hi [Name] — I've been following [specific, real thing about their work — a piece they wrote, a technique they're known for]. I built Aureon, an astrology/numerology platform, and just finished the piece I think matters most to someone doing client work: one place for natal, Vedic, solar return, progressions, composite/Davison charts, plus a real client roster and PDF reports you can hand someone.
>
> I'm not trying to replace the judgment you bring to a reading — just trying to save you the time of juggling three different calculators to get there. Would genuinely value five minutes of your read on whether it holds up for real client work, not a sales pitch. Happy to just set you up with a free look.

### 2. Post/intro for a professional association forum or Facebook group

> I'm the founder of Aureon, an astrology/numerology platform I've been building — I know unsolicited product posts aren't always welcome here, so take this as a straightforward "here's what it is, judge for yourself" rather than a pitch.
>
> It covers natal, Vedic (sidereal + nakshatras + current dasha), solar return, progressions, composite and Davison charts, every major house system, plus a client roster and PDF reports for anyone doing this for clients rather than just themselves. If anyone wants to actually kick the tires and tell me what's missing for real practice work, I'd rather hear the honest critique than a compliment — that's genuinely how it gets better.

### 3. Pitch to an astrology newsletter/podcast writer

> Subject: Would this be useful for [their newsletter/show name]'s audience?
>
> Hi [Name] — [specific reference to a recent piece/episode]. I built Aureon, a multi-system astrology/numerology platform, and I think it might genuinely interest your audience for two different reasons depending on who's listening: practitioners get a real working tool (client roster, every chart type, PDF reports); people just exploring their own chart get something closer to a persistent AI companion than a daily-horoscope reset.
>
> No ask attached to this — just offering an early look in case it's worth covering, or worth telling me honestly it isn't there yet.

## What NOT to do

- **Don't mass-DM.** A cold-outreach campaign at volume in a small professional community will be noticed and will burn the relationship before it starts — this only works as genuinely personalized, low-volume outreach.
- **Don't claim calculation superiority over astro-seek or any established tool** — both run on the same underlying astronomy; the honest pitch is consolidation and modern tooling, not "more accurate."
- **Don't offer free Practitioner accounts at a volume that undermines the $99/mo price** before there's any signal on whether the pitch even lands — a handful of genuine early testers is different from a giveaway.
- **Don't send anything from this document as-is.** Every draft above needs the bracketed specifics filled in with something real about the actual recipient, or it reads as a template — which it is, until Gio makes it not one.
